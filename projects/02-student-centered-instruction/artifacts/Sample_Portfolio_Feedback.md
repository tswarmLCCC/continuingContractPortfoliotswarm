## 1. Student Evaluation Excerpts on the Portfolio Model

### Case Study A: Neurodivergent Learner (Reduced Test Anxiety)
> *"I have severe testing anxiety when it comes to timed programming tests. The mini-portfolio format completely changed how I approach computer science. Knowing that I could just sit down and talk with Trevor about my code instead of staring at a ticking timer allowed me to actually enjoy writing the software."*  
> — *COSC 2409 Student Evaluation*

### Case Study B: Working Adult Learner (Flexible Video Submission)
> *"Because I work full-time during the day, being able to record my Loom video explanation at 9:00 PM on my own schedule was a lifesaver. Trevor's video feedback was detailed and encouraging—he pointed out ways I could simplify my list comprehension in three minutes of screen recording that made more sense than the whole chapter."*  
> — *COSC 1030 Student Evaluation*
