> **Focus:** Responsive Teaching & Curricular Evolution  
> **Target Courses:** COSC 1010, COSC 1030, COSC 2409

## 1. Student Feedback vs. Instructional Interventions

The table below contrasts constructive student critique regarding third-party courseware (ZyBooks) with the actionable instructional solutions implemented by Trevor Swarm:

| Student Critique / Friction Point | Student Quote Evidence | Instructional Adaptation Implemented | Long-Term Strategic Outcome |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Monotony & Reading Fatigue** | *"The zyBooks reading felt very repetitive and took hours to click through every single box."* | **Syllabus Re-weighting:** Reduced automated participation weight from 25% to 10%; excused optional repetitive modules. | Prioritizes student energy on creative problem-solving rather than rote point-clicking. |
| **Rigid Automated Syntax Grading** | *"The autograder failed my code because I had an extra space at the end of a line, even though the math was right."* | **Mini Programming Portfolios:** Replaced high-stakes automated lab submissions with human-centered verbal defenses (video or live). | Eliminates syntax panic; fosters deep computational comprehension and confidence. |
| **Lack of Conceptual Context** | *"Sometimes the textbook dives straight into syntax without explaining why anyone would use this feature."* | **Topical Micro-Lectures:** Recorded 5-minute pre-lecture video guides breaking down real-world scenarios before students open the text. | Cognitive scaffolding: provides a mental model before technical reading begins. |
| **Textbook Cost & Accessibility** | *"Paying $85 for an access code each semester is tough when money is tight."* | **OER Transition Initiative:** Began drafting modular, open-source programming curriculum materials hosted in Markdown and GitHub. | Eliminates course material costs entirely while tailoring content to Wyoming workforce standards. |
