	1.  Videos designed to be consumed at any time
	2.  Resource that checks learning as you read

	1.  Advanced students can work ahead, have full access to the final and all materials, and help lead weekly discussions
	2.  I lecture in my office hours on-demand whenever students need it, and also take requests for video calls at any time I am free, including nights and weekends
