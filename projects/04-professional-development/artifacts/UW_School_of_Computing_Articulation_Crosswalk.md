# Artifact 4.2: University of Wyoming School of Computing Articulation Crosswalk

> **Partnership:** Laramie County Community College & University of Wyoming (UW)  
> **Participating Units:** LCCC Business & Technology Division & UW School of Computing  
> **Faculty Lead:** Trevor Swarm  

---

## 1. 2+2 Articulation Objective

To provide LCCC Computer Science (AS) and Artificial Intelligence (AAS) graduates with guaranteed, friction-free transfer pathways into the University of Wyoming's Bachelor of Science in Computing or related technical degree programs.

## 3. Collaborative Institutional Outcomes

* **Zero Credit Loss:** Guarantees that LCCC students completing their associate degree enter UW with junior standing in the School of Computing.
* **Curriculum Synchronization:** Annual meetings between LCCC and UW faculty ensure prerequisite topics (such as pointer handling in C++ and linear algebra concepts in machine learning) are uniformly covered.
