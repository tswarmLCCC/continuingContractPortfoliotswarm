# Artifact 4.1: Intel & Dell "AI for the Workforce" Curriculum Integration Matrix

> **Initiative:** National AI for the Workforce Program (Intel & Dell Technologies)  
> **Institutional Lead:** Trevor Swarm  
> **Target Degree:** Associate of Applied Science in Artificial Intelligence  

---

## 1. Initiative Overview

In 2023–2024, Laramie County Community College joined Intel and Dell's national *AI for the Workforce* initiative. This partnership equips community colleges to deploy enterprise-grade AI education through certified course materials, specialized hardware specifications, and faculty professional development.

## 2. Curricular Alignment Matrix

| Intel / Dell Competency Module | LCCC Course Alignment | Practical Lab Implementation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Demystifying AI & Human-Centric AI** | `AIML 1010`: Introduction to AI | Non-technical analysis of AI capabilities, prompt engineering, and ethical impact assessment. |
| **Data-Centric AI & Preprocessing** | `COSC 2060`: Data Engineering | Cleaning tabular datasets, feature engineering, data imputation, and SQL database pipelines. |
| **Computer Vision & Convolutional Nets** | `AIML 2200`: Computer Vision | OpenCV workflows, real-time webcam inference, object detection, and automated inspection scripts. |
| **Natural Language Processing & LLMs** | `AIML 2300`: Natural Language Processing | Text classification, sentiment analysis, tokenization, embeddings, and localized RAG architecture. |
| **Edge Computing & Model Optimization** | `AIML 2900`: Applied AI Capstone | Model quantization and deployment onto localized edge hardware (Intel OpenVINO toolkit). |
