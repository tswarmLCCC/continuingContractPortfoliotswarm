## 1. Initiative Overview

To ensure Laramie County Community College's artificial intelligence curriculum meets real-world enterprise standards, LCCC partnered with Intel and Dell Technologies through the national **"AI for the Workforce"** program. 

Through this initiative, I participated in technical training sprints focused on:
1. **Data-Centric AI:** Modern data preparation, cleaning pipelines, and bias auditing.
2. **Computer Vision & Edge Inference:** Utilizing Intel OpenVINO toolkit for optimized edge-computing model inference.
3. **Applied Machine Learning:** Deploying scalable model architectures on standard workstation and edge hardware.

## 3. Impact on Student Readiness

* **Zero-Cost High-Value Toolsets:** Students gain hands-on experience with enterprise-grade open-source toolkits (OpenVINO, Scikit-Learn, OpenCV) without expensive licensing fees.
* **Workforce Credibility:** Curriculum alignment allows students to demonstrate proficiency against competencies vetted by international technology leaders.
